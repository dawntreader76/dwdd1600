Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers include:

John Hancock
President of the Second Continental Congress, celebrated for his large, bold signature placed squarely in the center of the document.
Thomas Jefferson
Principal author of the Declaration, member of the Committee of Five, and later the third President of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
The oldest signer at age 70, a renowned polymath and diplomat who assisted in editing the draft and later secured French military support.
John Adams
Fierce advocate for independence on the Congress floor, Committee of Five member, and later the second President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
Leader of the Sons of Liberty and organizer of the Boston Tea Party who helped rally early popular resistance against British rule.
Richard Henry Lee
Virginia delegate who introduced the formal Lee Resolution on June 7, 1776, proposing complete independence from Great Britain.
Benjamin Rush
Prominent physician, educator, and social reformer known as the "Father of American Psychiatry."
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
The only Roman Catholic signer and the last surviving member among all 56 signers, living until 1832.
Roger Sherman
Committee of Five member and the only individual to sign all four founding documents of the United States.
